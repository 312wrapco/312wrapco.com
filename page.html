How a wrap actually happens

Five steps, about four days.

Every wrap runs the same sequence. Skipping any one of them is why wraps fail at eighteen months instead of lasting seven years.

01

Measure & template

We photograph and measure your actual vehicle rather than trusting a stock template. Rivets, door gaps, hinges and body lines all get mapped before anything is designed.

02

Design & proof

Your artwork gets laid onto that template so you see where the door handle lands in your logo. You approve a proof of every side before we touch a printer.

03

Print & laminate

Printed on 3M cast film, then overlaminated for UV and abrasion resistance. Cast, never calendered — calendered vinyl shrinks and lifts on compound curves.

04

Install

Clean bay, controlled temperature, panel by panel. Surfaces are degreased and the vehicle is brought to temperature so the adhesive sets the way it is supposed to.

05

Post-heat & inspect

Every recess and rivet gets post-heated so the film keeps its memory and stays down. Then a walkaround, a care guide, and you drive it out.
